# Script template

`script.md` for the winning idea. Short-form craft rules this encodes: the hook has three layers
(visual, on-screen text, sound; `hooks.md`) and all three land in the first second; something new
happens every 3 to 5 seconds; the payoff lands inside the first third; then a call to action; the
piece works with the sound off.

```
# Script: <title>

Platform: <platform(s)>   Length: <seconds>   Tag: RENDER | FILM
Idea: <id>   Source decision: decision.md

## Hook (0-3s)
- Visual: <what frame 1 shows: a concrete subject, already moving, the result or the odd thing; never a title card, logo or empty scene>
- On screen: <text at 0 s, max 12 words; on the artwork about 8 or fewer>
- Spoken / sound: <the first-second line, max 12 words, or the sound that belongs to the action; "none" only on a photo slideshow>
- Product name in the hook: never (text, spoken, caption's first line)

## Beats
| # | Time | Visual | On-screen text | Spoken / audio | What changes |
|---|------|--------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| 1 | 3-6s | ... | ... | ... | <the new beat or pattern interrupt> |
| 2 | 6-10s | ... | ... | ... | ... |

## Payoff
<the line or moment that delivers the hook's promise, with time; it lands inside the first third of the piece>

## Comment trigger
<one planted, honest reason to comment or send: a choice to argue about, a question only the viewer can answer, or a deliberate small omission. Never engagement bait ("comment YES", "tag a friend").>

## Loop
<only for a piece of 15 s or less: how the last frame flows back into the first (the last word completes the first line, the end state is the start state); "none" otherwise>

## CTA
<one action; use the kit's CTA claim or an approved line where there is one>

## Publish copy
Per platform the brief asks for. The keyword the audience would search for sits in the caption's
opening words (and in the on-screen text and speech); the first line carries the hook.
- TikTok: <caption; keep the visible part to about 150 characters>
- Instagram: <caption; first line carries the hook, max 125 characters shown before "more">
- YouTube Shorts: <title max 100 characters>
- Facebook: <caption>
- Hashtags: <3 to 5, no more>
- First comment: <text, or "none"; posted by hand, because Conductor cannot post comments>
- Alternate hooks (spare hooks of other types for later pieces with their own bodies; never the same body re-posted on one account and platform):
  1. <hook line> (<hookType>)
  2. <hook line> (<hookType>)
  3. <optional>

Hook types: question, bold-claim, pattern-interrupt, relatable-pain, how-to, number,
curiosity-gap, story, social-proof (tag from `voice.md`'s shapes table).

## Copy-rule check
<each must-say / must-avoid item from brief.md, and ok or the fix>
## Voice check
<each line changed against voice.md, the hook included: before, after, which question or tell it failed; or "all lines pass". Functional lines (CTA, save or follow ask, link in bio, pinned comment, plain app mention, disclosure, approved lines) are checked only against "Tells to cut". Wording the person asked for stays; note any tell it trips here.>
```

Rules: the payoff time falls inside the first third of the length; a beat every 3 to 5 seconds (a `MOTION` idea instead has one headline, one optional body and an end card, with no timed on-screen beats); no beat longer than 5 seconds without a visual or text change;
caption limits are in `platform-specs.md`; alternates differ from the main hook in hook type, not just wording (check current platform guidance); approved lines run
verbatim; leave out platforms the brief did not ask for.
