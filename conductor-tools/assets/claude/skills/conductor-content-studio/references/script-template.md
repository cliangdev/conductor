# Script template

`script.md` for the winning idea. Short-form craft rules this encodes: the hook lands in 0 to 3
seconds, spoken and on screen; something new happens every 3 to 5 seconds; there is a payoff, then
a call to action; the piece works with the sound off.

```
# Script: <title>

Platform: <platform(s)>   Length: <seconds>   Tag: RENDER | FILM
Idea: <id>   Source decision: decision.md

## Hook (0-3s)
- Spoken: <line, max 12 words, or "none">
- On screen: <text, max 12 words>
- Visual: <what is shown>

## Beats
| # | Time | Visual | On-screen text | Spoken / audio | What changes |
|---|------|--------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| 1 | 3-6s | ... | ... | ... | <the new beat or pattern interrupt> |
| 2 | 6-10s | ... | ... | ... | ... |

## Payoff
<the line or moment that delivers the hook's promise, with time>

## CTA
<one action; use the kit's CTA claim or an approved line where there is one>

## Captions
- TikTok: <text; the first line carries the hook, keep the visible part to about 150 characters>
- Instagram: <text, first line carries the hook, max 125 characters shown before "more">
- YouTube Shorts: <title max 100 characters>
- Facebook: <text>
- Hashtags: <3 to 5, no more>

## Copy-rule check
<each must-say / must-avoid item from brief.md, and ok or the fix>
```

Rules: a beat every 3 to 5 seconds; no beat longer than 5 seconds without a visual or text change;
caption limits are in `platform-specs.md` (check current platform guidance); approved lines run
verbatim; leave out platforms the brief did not ask for.
